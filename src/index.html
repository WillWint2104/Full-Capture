    1. A 3-2-1 countdown gives you a moment to get ready. Press Esc to cancel.

      How Full Capture works

      Everything happens on this computer. No account, no upload.

      1. 1
        Set up. Name the lesson, turn on your microphone and choose the screen you’ll teach from. Check my sound sets your mic level for you.
      2. 2
        Record. The floating controls stay on top of your slides. Pause whenever you need to, and add a chapter at each new topic.
      3. 3
        Review. Play the take back, fix the name, copy chapters for YouTube, or record another take.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Alt + RStart recording · Stop & save
      Alt + PPause · Resume
      Alt + MAdd chapter
      EscCancel the countdown · close a window like this one

      Shortcuts work here and in the floating controls, but not while you’re typing.

      If something goes wrong

      A safety copy is kept while you record. If the browser or computer closes mid-lesson, open Full Capture again and choose Save it on the card at the top.
