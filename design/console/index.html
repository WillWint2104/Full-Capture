      Help

      How it works

      1. 1
        Set up. Name the lesson, turn on your microphone and run Check my sound, then choose your screen.
      2. 2
        Record. The floating controls stay on top of your slides, with the timer, Pause, Add chapter and Stop & save.
      3. 3
        Review. Play the take back, rename it, copy YouTube chapters, or record another take.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Alt + RStart recording · Stop & save
      Alt + PPause · Resume
      Alt + MAdd chapter
      EscCancel the countdown · close a window

      They work here and in the floating controls, but not while you’re typing.

      Your recordings are safe

      Safety copy: every second is also kept on this computer while you record. If the browser or computer closes unexpectedly, Full Capture offers to save the recording the next time you open it.

      Private: it works offline. Nothing is uploaded.

      Not included

      Cursor highlights, drawing on the screen, background blur, uploading or share links. Use your usual tools for those.
