    How to fix this in Windows
    1. Right-click the speaker icon at the bottom-right of the taskbar and choose Sound settings.
    2. Under Input, pick Headset Microphone (Jabra Evolve2 30).
    3. Set Input volume to about 90.
    4. Open More sound settings › Recording › your headset › Properties › Levels, and set Microphone Boost to 0 dB.
    1. Right-click the speaker icon on the taskbar and choose Open Sound settings.

        How Full Capture works

        1. 1
          Name your lesson. The name becomes the file name.
        2. 2
          Turn on your microphone and press Check my sound. We set your mic level for you.
        3. 3
          Choose your screen. Switch on Share system audio if you’ll play videos.
        4. 4
          Press Start recording. The floating controls stay on top while you teach.

        Keyboard shortcuts

        Alt + RStart recording / Stop & save
        Alt + PPause / Resume
        Alt + MAdd chapter
        EscCancel the countdown, close a window like this one

        Shortcuts work here and in the floating controls, but not while you’re typing.

        Good to know

        • Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.
        • A safety copy is kept while you record. If the browser crashes, open Full Capture again and we’ll offer to save it.
        • If your headset comes unplugged, recording carries on and the sound comes back when you plug it in.
        • Choose a folder (top of the page) and takes are saved straight into it while you record.
